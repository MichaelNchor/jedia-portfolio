Profile

Precision, by habit

I came to AI data work from a plant floor, where a decimal point is the difference between yield and waste.

  • Based inAccra, Ghana
  • FocusComputer vision data ops
  • DegreeB.Sc. Chemical Engineering
  • CertifiedAWS Cloud Practitioner
  • ModalitiesRGB · Thermal/IR · LiDAR
  • StatusAvailable, remote or onsite

Chemical engineering taught me to instrument a process, log it honestly and chase the root cause rather than the symptom. That habit transfers directly to training data: every mislabelled frame is a defect that compounds downstream, and every ambiguous guideline is a process gap waiting to be closed.

Today I annotate high-resolution infrared and thermal imagery from marine security vessels, run multi-object tracking across high-fps video, and place 3D bounding boxes on LiDAR and depth-sensor data. Alongside the labelling I audit other people's work, cataloguing edge cases, tightening taxonomies and feeding fixes back to project leads so the whole pipeline gets sharper, not just my own queue.

I hold an AWS Cloud Practitioner certification and work comfortably in Python, MATLAB and heavily-modelled Excel, which means I can reason about the systems my datasets end up feeding.

I work remotely with teams across time zones, and onsite with teams in Accra. If you need a labelling queue held to a standard, I am reachable at jamoaba1@gmail.com.
